# Bible texts used by the sermon captions demo

Verse-per-line exports from eBible.org, stored as `BOOK C:V<TAB>text`, gzipped.
The texts are unmodified apart from removing paragraph marks, and, in the KJV,
the square brackets that mark translators' supplied words.
